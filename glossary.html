Definitions

Click any metric in the results to jump here.

CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate)

Annualized growth rate over the backtest period. Equivalent constant yearly rate that matches the total return.

Total Return

Cumulative return over the period: (Ending Equity / Starting Equity) − 1.

Max Drawdown (MDD)

Largest peak-to-trough decline in equity curve (usually expressed as a percentage).

Sharpe Ratio

Risk-adjusted return: mean(excess returns) / std(returns). (Specify your return frequency and risk-free assumption.)

Signal (-1, 0, +1)

Trading instruction per strategy: -1 sell, 0 hold, +1 buy. Your aggregated stock signal is sign(sum of signals).

Cooldown Bars

Minimum number of bars to wait between trades/signals to reduce churn and overtrading.

Window

Lookback period used by an indicator (e.g., RSI 14 means RSI uses the last 14 bars).
